What is Inside Our Server?

To keep things organized and helpful, we have divided our server into specific channels tailored to your needs:

  • #💡-general-chat: The main lobby where members discuss tech trends, share memes, and network.
  • #🆘-coding-help: Stuck on a bug? Drop your broken code here and get instant debugging help from peers and mentors.
  • #🚀-showcase: Built something cool? Drop links to your live websites, games, or open-source projects here to get feedback.
  • #📢-announcements: Stay updated with our weekly hackathons, guest speaker events, and course drops.
  • #🤖-ai-and-bots: A dedicated playground where students can test, build, and share custom Discord bots.

Server Community Rules

To ensure a safe and productive environment for all young creators, all members must agree to our core guidelines upon entering:

  1. Be Respectful: No harassment, bullying, or hate speech. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy.
  2. No Spamming: Keep discussions relevant to the specific channels. Do not flood chats with promotional links.
  3. Collaborate Freely: Help others when you can. We grow much faster when we learn together.
  4. Safe Environment: No inappropriate, adult, or illegal content sharing.
